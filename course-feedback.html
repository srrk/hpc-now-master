Operations readiness The north star for this programme is 90-day ops readiness — not conceptual completeness. The feedback question that matters most is whether you can now handle HPC infrastructure tasks independently, or whether you are closer to that point than when you started.
What to cut Every module that could be shorter is time that could go to something more useful. If something felt like it was running long for no reason, that is the most actionable thing you can tell the next cohort.
